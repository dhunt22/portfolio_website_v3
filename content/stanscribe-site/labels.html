Labels and Markings

Updated November 19, 2026

European Union law requires some products to carry labels or markings, such as the CE marking. This page says which of them apply to Stan Scribe and Stan Scribe Light.

Stan Scribe

Stan Scribe is software for iPhone. It is available only as a download on the App Store, and it has no packaging and no physical parts.

No label or marking required by European Union law applies to Stan Scribe. It carries no CE marking.

Stan Scribe is not a medical device. It shows captions of speech and does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition.

Stan Scribe Light

Stan Scribe Light is software for iPhone. It is available only as a download on the App Store, and it has no packaging and no physical parts.

No label or marking required by European Union law applies to Stan Scribe Light. It carries no CE marking.

Stan Scribe Light is not a medical device. It shows captions of speech and does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition.

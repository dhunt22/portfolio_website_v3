Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Stan Scribe Light is made by Devin Hunt ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with information. If you have a question, email stanscribe@devinhunt.com.

The short version

  • Stan Scribe Light does not collect any data. I never see your captions, your conversations, your voice or anything else you do in the app.
  • Everything the app makes stays on your device unless you choose otherwise. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app itself never connects to the internet.
  • I don't sell or share anyone's personal information, and nothing from the app is used for advertising.
  • If you turn on iCloud backup, iOS keeps the text of your conversations in your own iCloud, so your other devices can import them. I can't see it. Turn it off and delete the "Stan Scribe Light" data in iCloud settings to remove it.
  • Sound from the microphone is turned into captions as you listen. The sound itself is never saved and never sent anywhere.

What the app keeps on your device

Sound from the microphone. While captions are on, the app listens through the microphone and turns speech into text on your device. The sound is used only in memory, a few seconds at a time, and is never written to storage. Captions keep running when you switch to another app or lock your iPhone, until you tap Stop. While the app is listening, iOS shows the microphone indicator, and the app shows a Captioning card on the Lock Screen. The card shows only how long captions have been on and how many people have spoken, never words or names. You can turn it off in your iPhone's Settings app.

Saved conversations. The words, the names of speakers, your bookmarks, highlights and titles are saved in the app's storage on your device so you can read them later. They are kept until you delete them or delete the app, and they are protected by your device passcode. Like other app data, they are included in your device's iCloud or computer backups if you use backups. You can delete any conversation in the app. With iCloud backup on, deleting a conversation also deletes its iCloud copy.

Voice profiles (known voices). If you set up a person's voice, the app listens to that person speak for about half a minute and turns their voice into a set of numbers, a "voiceprint", that helps the app recognize them later and show their name. The sound is discarded as soon as the voiceprint is made. Voiceprints:

  • stay on your device, are protected by your device passcode, and are excluded from backups;
  • are used only to show names in your own captions;
  • are never sent to me or anyone else;
  • are kept until you remove them (People → the person → Forget this voice) or delete the app.

A voiceprint can identify a person, and some laws treat it as biometric information. Only set up a voice for someone who has agreed to it.

Summaries and topics. On devices with Apple Intelligence, the app can write a short summary of a saved conversation using Apple's on-device model. This happens on your device. Other titles, topics and places come from the words in the conversation, also on your device.

Settings. Your choices, such as text size and appearance, are saved on your device.

Lock Screen and widget. If you add the widget, it can show the names of the people in your last conversation and how long it lasted. Anyone who can see your Lock Screen can read it. You can turn this off in Settings (Show last conversation).

What leaves your device

Only what you send. When you share or export a conversation, turn on Obsidian sync, or turn on iCloud backup, the conversation's text goes to the app, folder or service you choose. For iCloud backup that is your own iCloud account, and each copy holds a conversation's words, speaker names, highlights, titles and summary, but no sound and no voiceprints. What happens to it there is up to that app or service and its own privacy policy.

Beta versions

If you test a beta of Stan Scribe Light through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with me what TestFlight collects, such as crash reports, how often the beta is used, the feedback and screenshots you choose to send, and, if I invited you by email, your name and email address. I use it only to fix and improve the app. Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy cover that data.

Crash reports

The app sends no crash reports itself. If you turn on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with me. Apple controls that setting and that data.

If you email me

If you email stanscribe@devinhunt.com, I get your email address and whatever you write. I use them only to answer you. I don't add you to any list and I don't share your email. Ask, and I'll delete our emails.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

Apart from emails you send me, I hold no data about you, so there is nothing for me to look up, correct, hand over or delete. You control everything on your device: delete a conversation or a voice in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from the device. Copies in your backups or in places you exported to are yours to manage.

The app doesn't track you, so it does nothing differently when your device or browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and at https://devinhunt.com/stanscribe-light/privacy/, with a new effective date. If a change ever means the app would collect data, it will ask you first.
